About Us

Wannatie is an e-commerce platform for furniture and statement pieces, built to connect thoughtful products with the people looking for them.

We work with local brands and independent makers to create a space where customers can buy, sell, and explore with confidence. Our focus is simple - quality products, fair value, and clear curation.

We believe the right piece does more than fill a space. It shapes the environment around it and naturally becomes part of how a home is experienced.

Wannatie exists to make meaningful design more accessible, practical, and trusted for both creators and customers.

Progress

Wannatie is currently in active development.

The platform's frontend experience has been built and is now being structured for scalability and long-term growth. Core backend development is in progress, with the database architecture already prepared to support upcoming functionality.

The Wannatie identity, domain, logo, and social platforms have been established as part of building a trusted ecosystem around thoughtful commerce and design.

Wannatie is currently being developed independently, and meaningful contributions, ideas, and collaborations are always welcome.
